# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React 18, prerendered at build to static HTML, deployed on GitHub Pages (migrated from plain HTML/CSS/JS at the user's request on 2026-10-05).

## Users

Primary: couples getting married who arrive from the Instagram profile link, on a phone. Secondary: wedding planners who want to offer the service to their clients. Both decide quickly and contact by Instagram DM.

## Product Purpose

"Webs de Boda | Invitaciones Web" sells a personalised wedding website (online invitation + day information + RSVP in a single link). This corporate page exists only to convert: a visitor understands the offer in 10 seconds, sees the real demos and writes by Instagram DM (@webs.de.boda).

## Positioning

One freelance developer (Nacho) designs and programs every web by hand, one-to-one, fully customisable (typography, palette, photos, texts, sections). Demos are only a starting point. A template marketplace cannot truthfully claim that.

## Operating Context

Traffic comes from Instagram, Facebook and cold emails to wedding planners. Contact channel is Instagram DM only; no public phone or email exists yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Included: countdown, couple story, ceremony and celebration with times and map, gallery, guest info (transport, lodging, dress code), gift table with copyable IBAN, RSVP (plus-ones, allergies, favourite song), FAQ, collaborative playlist.
- Price: 50 EUR launch offer, regular 100 EUR. No end date, no scarcity claims.
- Process: 1 choose style, 2 send data and photos, 3 web ready in a few days.
- Undecided / do not invent: domain, hosting, number of revisions, payment methods, extra contact channels, Facebook URL.
- Copy: Spanish (Spain), informal "tu", short concrete sentences, no emojis.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Webs de Boda | Invitaciones Web"; logo of two interlocked golden rings (source PNG at ../ImagenesRedesBodas/00-logo/logo-perfil-1080.png, to be vectorised). No other brand or company name visible. Starting palette in the brief: cream #F4EBDD, gold #B8924A, dark brown #3A2E22, soft brown #78644E; editorial serif for headlines, clean sans for text. Avoid generic wedding-template look (clipart flowers, illegible scripts, pink gradients) and SaaS-landing clichés.

## Evidence on Hand

Four real demos: Elegante, Moderna, Boho, Tendencia 2026 (GitHub Pages URLs). No testimonials, client counts, logos, awards or reviews exist; none may be shown.

## Product Principles

1. Convert first: the offer and the Instagram action are legible within one viewport on a phone.
2. Show the real thing: live demos, never invented screenshots.
3. Honest and close: tú-a-tú voice, no invented proof or urgency.
4. Nothing hides if JS fails.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Contrast AA, visible focus, keyboard navigation, alt text, prefers-reduced-motion respected. Mobile-first (360 to 1440 px).
